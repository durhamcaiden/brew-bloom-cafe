About Brew & Bloom

Our Story

Brew & Bloom Café was founded in 2018 by longtime friends and coffee enthusiasts Maya Rivera and James Holloway. After years of working in specialty coffee shops across Chicago and St. Louis, they decided to bring their passion for craft coffee to their college town of Carbondale, Illinois.

The name "Brew & Bloom" reflects their philosophy: just as plants grow when given the right environment, people flourish when given a welcoming, nourishing space. The café was designed to be a community hub — not just a coffee stop.

Today, Brew & Bloom employs 12 local team members, serves over 300 guests daily, and has become a beloved landmark on the Southern Illinois University campus strip.

Our Mission

We are committed to three core values:

  • Sourcing fair-trade, ethically traded coffee beans from small family farms
  • Reducing our environmental footprint through compostable packaging and zero food waste programs
  • Creating an inclusive, safe, and welcoming environment for every guest

Certifications & Partnerships

  • Certified by the Specialty Coffee Association (SCA)
  • Official fair-trade partner with Equal Exchange Co-op
  • Local food bank partner — 5% of all pastry sales donated weekly
  • Proud sponsor of SIU student events and local art shows
